BrightLeaf Solar Remote Work and Equipment Policy

Approved remote employees are responsible for protecting company equipment and
data while working outside a BrightLeaf office.

Remote work requirements:
- Connect to internal tools using the company VPN.
- Do not store sensitive files on personal devices.
- Keep laptops physically secure during travel and at home.
- Use only company-approved collaboration and file-sharing tools.
- Notify IT immediately if a device is lost, stolen, or believed to be
  compromised.

This policy applies to U.S.-based employees whose roles are approved for remote
or hybrid work arrangements.
