BrightLeaf Solar Security Policies

BrightLeaf Solar maintains the following core security requirements:
- Multi-factor authentication is required for email, VPN, payroll, and all
  cloud business systems.
- Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and automatic screen lock after
  10 minutes of inactivity.
- Employees must use the approved password manager and may not share passwords.
- Access to customer and employee data is granted by role and reviewed
  quarterly.
- Security incidents, suspicious emails, and lost devices must be reported to
  the security team immediately.
- Software updates and critical patches must be installed as soon as practical.
