BrightLeaf Solar Employee Benefits Guide

Full-time employees at BrightLeaf Solar are eligible for a benefits package that
includes:
- Medical insurance
- Dental insurance
- Vision insurance
- A 401(k) retirement plan with a 4% company match
- Fifteen days of paid time off each year
- Ten paid company holidays
- Twelve weeks of paid parental leave for eligible employees
- Annual professional development reimbursement of up to $1,500

Benefits eligibility begins on the first day of the month after an employee's
start date unless otherwise noted in a signed offer letter.
