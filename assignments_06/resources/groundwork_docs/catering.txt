Groundwork Coffee Co. Catering

Groundwork offers catering and delivery for small-scale events in the Los
Angeles area.

Catering items include:
- Drip coffee
- Hand-crafted food menu items
- Fresh baked goods
- Small bites

Important details:
- Delivery options are available at select Groundwork locations.
- Groundwork requires a minimum of 48 hours before the expected delivery time.
- For catering inquiries, customers should contact their local Groundwork cafe.

This service is intended for guests who need hot, fresh, organic coffee and
bites catered to them.
