Groundwork Coffee Co. Menu Notes

Groundwork cafes serve handcrafted organic coffee drinks, espresso beverages,
tea, fresh baked goods, pastries, salads, sandwiches, and small bites.

Common beverage categories include:
- Drip coffee
- Espresso drinks
- Cold brew
- Tea
- Seasonal beverages

Dairy-free milk options:
- Oat milk
- Almond milk
- House-made organic cashew milk

Plant-based options:
- Oat milk
- Almond milk
- House-made organic cashew milk

The cafes also offer vegan-friendly options in parts of the menu. If a guest
asks whether dairy-free milk is available, the answer is yes. If someone asks
"Do you offer any dairy-free milk options?" the answer is yes, and the listed
options are oat milk, almond milk, and house-made organic cashew milk.
