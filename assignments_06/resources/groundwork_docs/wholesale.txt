Groundwork Coffee Co. Wholesale

Groundwork offers wholesale coffee service for businesses that want to serve
Groundwork products.

Wholesale customers may include:
- Restaurants
- Hotels
- Cafes
- Grocery stores

What Groundwork provides:
- Certified organic coffee and tea
- Guidance on beverage service
- Equipment recommendations
- Store layout and design consultation
- Barista training

Groundwork describes itself as one of the West Coast's larger specialty coffee
roasters and distributors and supports partners who want an organic and
sustainable coffee program.
