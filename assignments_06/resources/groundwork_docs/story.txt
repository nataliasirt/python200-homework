Groundwork Coffee Co. Story

Groundwork's mission is to source and share exceptional organic coffee that
inspires people to work hard, dream big, and impact the world.

Company background:
- Groundwork opened its first cafe in Venice Beach.
- The team started with a humble goal: share the best organic coffee they could
  find with the local community.
- Demand grew quickly, and Groundwork began roasting coffee 24/7 out of its
  tiny cafe on Rose Avenue to keep up.
- The company later expanded along the West Coast and now operates busy
  roasteries in Los Angeles and Portland.

Groundwork emphasizes certified organic coffee, strong local community ties,
and close involvement in the process from farms to co-ops to cafes.
