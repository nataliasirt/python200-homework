Groundwork Coffee Co. Loyalty Program

Groundwork Rewards is the cafe loyalty program for regular customers.

How it works:
- Customers can join the loyalty program online or in the cafe.
- Members earn 1 point for every $1 spent on qualifying cafe purchases.
- After reaching 100 points, members can redeem the reward for a free drink.
- Members also receive occasional promotional offers and a birthday reward.

Program reminders:
- Customers should give the phone number or account tied to their rewards
  profile at checkout so the visit counts toward points.
- Rewards and promotions may change over time, so store staff can confirm the
  latest details if needed.
